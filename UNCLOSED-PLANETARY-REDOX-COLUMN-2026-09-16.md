## 2. What already exists (checked first)

### Outer space
- Ethan Siegel, *Big Think*, 1 Dec 2025: nine remaining gaps in cosmic history — inflation’s trigger, inflation’s flavor, baryogenesis, dark-matter identity, first (Pop III) stars unobserved as of mid-2026, life’s commonality, abiogenesis, dark energy’s nature, cosmic fate.
- DESI 2024–2025: dark energy may be evolving (peaked ~2 Gyr ago). Not settled.
- Quanta, 22 Jun 2026: Vafa / Obied / Bedroya / Wu “dark dimension” couples dark matter and dark energy in string theory; consistent with DESI, **unproven**.
- arXiv:2606.19090 (ApJL 2026): axion-like early dark energy + Planck/ACT/SPT + DESI + JWST claims to reduce Hubble tension to ~1.0σ. **One model, not consensus.**
- LUX-ZEPLIN, announced ~1 Sep 2026 (DOE / *Scientific American* / AAAS): a single unexplained recoil (~248 keV) at 2.6σ (~0.5% chance of known background). **Not a discovery.** If real, implied mass ≳ 200 GeV — not a vanilla WIMP.
- Hubble / Holm et al., *Nature*, 16 Sep 2026: first candidate globular-cluster stellar stream beyond the Milky Way (“Oyashio”) around UGC 9050-Dw1, 115 million ly. New dark-matter probe. Spectroscopy of shared motion is still missing.
- NASA ExEP Science Gap List, Rev. H, Jul 2025: small-planet atmospheres, occurrence rates for temperate rocky worlds, complete biosignature / false-positive inventory still open.
- Teleios (ASKAP 2025): near-perfect radio bubble with no X-ray/optical counterpart. Shape without a story.

### Earth interior / land
- Yuan et al., *Nature Geoscience*, 15 Sep 2026: two previously unknown iron oxyhydroxides, **Fe5O12Hx** and **Fe7O12Hx**, stable in the lower mantle (660–2,900 km). Water is locked in minerals, not free liquid, and may sink toward ultralow-velocity zones at the core–mantle boundary. Quantity of water: **not determined.** Return flux to the surface: **unknown.** Wilson (Leeds): picture still “incomplete.”
- Eos, 15 Sep 2026: underground hydrogen — commercial bets racing the science. Microbial communities consume injected H2 at a few percent to >50% in field pilots. Diffuse degassing across ~2.5 million km² of serpentinized terrain is **essentially unmeasured** (0.02 to a few Mt/yr). Round-trip storage efficiency 25–40%.
- Geomagnetic reversals: dynamo is known; **what starts a reversal is not** (~250 kyr mean interval).

### Ocean
- Bell, Johannes, Kennedy, Poulton, *Science Advances* 2025, “How Little We’ve Seen”: **0.0006–0.001%** of the deep seafloor (≥200 m, ~335.7 million km²) visually observed. 2,130–3,823 km². ~44,000 dives since 1958. 65% of observations within 200 nm of the US, Japan, or New Zealand. 97% of dives by five countries. Even if the estimate is off by 10×, coverage is still <0.01%.
- Ocean Discovery League, 1 Apr 2026: Global Deep Sea Exploration Goals — 10,000 representative targets, aiming to roughly double unique sites ever seen.
- Peng, Du, Mordukhovich et al., *Nature* 645:679–685 (30 Jul 2025): chemosynthetic communities of siboglinid polychaetes and bivalves spanning **2,500 km** at **5,800–9,533 m** in the Kuril–Kamchatka and western Aleutian trenches, fed by fault-transported H2S and microbially produced methane. Authors: such communities “might be more widespread than previously anticipated.”
- Sweetman et al., *Nature Geoscience* 2024: “dark oxygen” — O2 rising in benthic chambers on polymetallic nodules in the Clarion–Clipperton Zone, in darkness. Mechanism unknown (abiotic electrochemistry, biochemistry, or other). **Disputed / under test.** Nippon Foundation DORI landers *Alisa* and *Kaia* deploying 2026 (UNESCO Ocean Decade).
- CCZ checklist (*Current Biology* / WoRDSS; reported 9 Sep 2026): **5,578** animal species recorded; **436** formally named (~7.8%); **88–92% unnamed**; only **six** found anywhere else. Zone area ~4.5 million km². 17 ISA exploration contracts. **No exploitation mining code as of March 2026.**
- Ocean Census: 1,121 new marine species in the last year, some to 6,575 m; estimate that most marine species remain undescribed.

### Animals / land life
- Vox / Hartop, 2 Sep 2025: ~90% of animals on Earth still undiscovered or unidentified (“dark taxa”). ~2 million described. This is an order-of-magnitude estimate, not a census.
- MIT / Shawar et al., *PNAS*, 29 Sep 2025: sponge-specific chemical fossils in Ediacaran rocks (>541 Ma) — demosponge ancestors among the first animals. Competing with other first-animal claims; not closed.
- Soil microbiome: the other undescribed majority, overlapping the natural-hydrogen terrains above.

### Humans
- Consciousness: still no consensus physical account (SciAm “impossible questions,” 15 Sep 2026).
- Sleep: competing functions, none sufficient.
- Caltech 2019 + Chae 2025 (Popular Mechanics, 4 Aug 2026): some humans show EEG changes to Earth-strength magnetic fields **without conscious report**. Chae: a firm causal link to mood or sleep **has not been demonstrated**.
- Gut magnetotactic bacteria associated with grey-matter volume in hippocampus / flocculonodular cerebellum (*J. Oceanol. Limnol.* 2021) — **association, not causation.**
- Microbial dark matter in the body: unmatched genes, uncultured majority; therapeutics already ship against that unnamed background (Whiteson / Discover, 2 Jun 2026).

### Icy moons
- Jan 2026: Europa’s seafloor is probably too quiet for Earth-like hydrothermal venting.
- Dec 2025 model: radiolytic splitting of water by radionuclides in seafloor salts could still supply H2 and O-bearing chemicals. Chemical energy ≠ biology. **Open.**

## 7. The genuine gap (not claimed as a finding of nature)

### Classification name
**Unclosed Planetary Redox Column (UPRC)**
paired with a split that the ranking forced into the open:

**Extractable-dark vs gravitational-dark.**

### What that means, in one paragraph
Independent fields named their unknown majority “dark” because they can see an effect and cannot name the thing. Cosmology’s dark remainder is only observed. The ocean’s, the soil’s, and the body’s dark remainders are already under contract, dose, or license. Earth’s hydrogen/oxygen/water chemistry is now reported at six depths — core–mantle minerals (15 Sep 2026), crustal H2 with a microbial sink (15 Sep 2026), hadal fault methane (2025), abyssal dark oxygen (2024, mechanism still unknown), surface photosynthesis (the only closed flux), and body water plus brain magnetite (association-level) — and **no published mass-balance couples them**. Europa is the quiet analog of the same unclosed column: radiolysis without vents. The unspoken fact is not “life is everywhere.” It is that **we have been running a 2-D mapping project on a 3-D redox object, and we are extracting the object from the middle while the top and bottom remain unbudgeted.**

### Per-realm, in individual senses (undiscovered / unspoken / unproved)

**Outer space — undiscovered.** The particle identity of dark matter; Pop III stars as of mid-2026; a confirmed biosignature above CoLD level 1; Teleios’s distance and nature.

**Outer space — unspoken.** DESI’s evolving dark energy and the “dark dimension” are being treated as separate from Earth’s unclosed water column. They should not be merged as substance. They *should* be noticed as the same epistemological move: infer an interior from a skin (expansion history, gravity, a mineral phase).

**Outer space — unproved.** LZ 2.6σ; EDE resolution of Hubble tension; dark-dimension coupling; Europa radiolysis as a habitable energy source; Oyashio as a confirmed stellar stream (no spectroscopy yet).

**Earth interior — undiscovered.** How much water Fe5O12Hx / Fe7O12Hx actually hold; what happens to that water at the CMB; the reversal starter of the dynamo.

**Earth interior — unspoken.** The water cycle did not stop at the hydrosphere. As of 15 Sep 2026 it has a candidate mineral floor. Mantle plumes, crustal H2, and hadal methane are still published as if they were different stories.

**Earth interior — unproved.** That the new oxyhydroxides close Earth’s water origin; that ULVZs *are* hydrated; that primordial magma-ocean water is the source.

**Land — undiscovered.** Diffuse natural-hydrogen flux; the soil’s unnamed microbial majority.

**Land — unspoken.** Hydrogen pilots are already losing tens of percent of injected gas to microbes whose communities were not in the engineering model. The land subsurface is an extractable-dark layer, not a geology footnote.

**Land — unproved.** Predictable suppression of the microbial H2 sink; commercial-scale geologic H2 traps.

**Ocean — undiscovered.** 99.999% of the deep seafloor unseen; most CCZ animals unnamed; dark-oxygen mechanism; how widespread hadal chemosynthesis is beyond two trenches.

**Ocean — unspoken.** Seventeen mining-exploration contracts sit on a seafloor whose animals are ~90% unnamed and whose oxygen source is under investigation. The inversion is the story, not the monsters.

**Ocean — unproved.** Dark oxygen as a real geochemical flux rather than an instrumental artifact; that nodules “powered early life”; that hadal communities are global.

**Animals — undiscovered.** Dark taxa (~90% estimate); sound-only cetaceans never seen; soil and midwater majorities.

**Animals — unspoken.** Taxonomy’s word “dark” is a loan from physics. The loan hides that these organisms are not invisible like WIMPs — they are uncollected. Different unknown.

**Animals — unproved.** That sponges were *the* first animals (sterane evidence is strong, not unique); that coiling flips in foraminifera are climate switches.

**Humans — unproved.** Unconscious magnetoreception affecting decisions, mood, or sleep (explicitly not demonstrated, Chae 2025/26); gut magnetotactic bacteria causing navigational brain structure.
